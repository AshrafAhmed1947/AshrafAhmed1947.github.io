Hi, I'm Ashraf Ahmed

I am currently pursuing a degree in Electrical and Electronic Engineering (EEE) at Southeast University, Dhaka. My research interests lie in photonics and RF engineering, including the design of photonic crystal fibers for mid-infrared supercontinuum generation and CSRR-integrated microstrip patch antennas for S-Band and C-Band applications, with work presented at international conferences in 2026. I have experience with simulation tools such as COMSOL Multiphysics, CST Studio Suite, ANSYS and MATLAB. Alongside my research, I am active in IEEE and robotics, serving as Coordinator (Technology & Digital) of the Southeast University IEEE Student Branch and Secretary (Robotics & Software) of the SEU EEE Club.
