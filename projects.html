IoT & Power Electronics Projects

IoT-Based Home Electricity Consumption Monitoring and Control System

Team Project

  • Developed an IoT-enabled system measuring real-time voltage, current, power and energy (kWh) using ZMPT101B voltage and ZMCT103C current sensors.
  • Calculated load consumption, detected abnormal conditions and implemented fault protection.
  • Transmitted data via ESP8266 to the cloud and displayed it on the Blynk app, enabling remote appliance control and efficient energy management.
ESP8266 Blynk ZMPT101B ZMCT103C Energy Monitoring

IoT-Enabled Hybrid Solar Inverter (200 W)

Team Project

  • Designed a hybrid inverter with solar input (18 to 50 V, 200 W max), 220 V grid input and LiFePO4 battery support (up to 100 Ah), delivering 200 W output.
  • Enabled direct solar-to-output operation and IoT-based remote monitoring.
  • Implemented overload, short-circuit and over-voltage protection.
